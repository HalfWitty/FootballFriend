Projections — How They Workread this first

Once a stats_2026_schedule.csv is loaded, 2026 shows up everywhere as a normal season – but until real games are played, most of its numbers are projected. Any figure that's still (partly) a projection is shown in orange text. The instant a real stats_2026_part1/2.csv row exists for a given player and week, it replaces the projection for that week automatically – nothing needs to be reloaded or recalculated by hand.

All projections are built only from 2024 and 2025 history (2022-2023 aren't used as a baseline). There are two projection engines:

1. Team-level: pass/rush attempts & pass %

Used on the Team tab's Pass/Run Tendency & Schedule table. For a team/week without real data yet:

  • Baseline = the team's own median pass attempts and median rush attempts, pooled across every 2024-2025 game they played.
  • Opponent defense factor = how much passing/rushing that week's opponent has historically allowed (median attempts by whoever played them, 2024-2025), divided by the league-wide average attempts per game.
  • Opponent matchup factor = the team's own attempts specifically in its 2024 and 2025 games against that exact opponent, compared to its baseline. This only nudges the projection if the direction is consistent in both years (both above baseline, or both below) – a mixed signal is treated as no pattern. One year of matchup data (instead of two) is applied at half strength.

The defense factor and matchup factor are averaged together, then applied to the baseline. Pass % is derived from the resulting projected pass/rush attempts.

2. Player-level: every individual stat

Used on the Player and Position tabs, and the Volume vs. Consistency charts. Two different methods depending on the stat:

  • Opportunity stats (targets, carries, pass attempts – whatever counts as "Opportunity" for that position, see the Position tab glossary below) = that week's team-level projected volume (from engine #1 above) × the player's own historical share of that volume. Share = median of (player's stat that week ÷ team's total for that stat that week), across all their 2024-2025 games.
  • Every other stat (yardage, touchdowns, receptions, fantasy points, etc.) = the player's own 2024-2025 median for that stat, adjusted by the same two-factor blend as the team engine: how much of that stat the opponent's defense has allowed to that position (vs. league average), averaged with the player's own consistent-direction history against that specific opponent.

A player only gets projected if they have some 2024-2025 history and their team's 2026 schedule is loaded. Rookies or players with zero 2024-2025 games won't show a projection.

Team Tabroster stats, tendency, schedule
Roster table

Rows are limited to players on that team's current (2026) roster – taken from each player's most recently loaded team assignment, not just "anyone who ever played there." Once a player is on the roster, all of their historical seasons are shown, even years accrued with a different team.

GP
Games played that season.
Total / Avg
Season total and per-game average for whichever stat is selected above.
Volatility %
Coefficient of variation: 100 × (standard deviation of weekly values) ÷ |mean of weekly values|. Lower = more consistent week to week; higher = more boom-or-bust. Requires at least 2 weeks of data.
Target Share / Carry Share charts

Each season gets its own bar (not an average across seasons): player's targets or carries that season ÷ the team's total targets or carries that season × 100. The 8 players shown are chosen by volume in the most recent season that actually has data for that team.

Pass/Run Tendency & Schedule table
Team Pass Att / Team Rush Att / Team Pass %
That week's actual (or, for 2026, projected) attempts and the resulting pass-attempt share of all offensive plays.
Opp Def Median Pass %
NOT the opponent's own passing tendency. It's the median pass % of the other teams that have played that opponent – i.e. what opposing offenses have typically done against that defense (a "pass funnel" indicator). For 2026 this blends real games played so far with 2024-2025 history for that same opponent.

Coloring: if Opp Def Median Pass % is lower than the team's own pass % that week, the Opp Def column is red and the Team Pass % column is green (and vice versa) – flagging weeks where a team's actual/projected pass rate ran against what that defense usually sees.

Scoped to 2024-2026 only; 2022-2023 aren't tracked for this table. The median line above the table covers 2024 & 2025 only.

Position Tabrankings, ADP/value, volume vs. consistency
Main season table
Opportunity
Position-specific volume, not points: QB = pass attempts + rush attempts · RB = carries + targets · WR/TE = targets · K = FG attempts + XP attempts.
Opp Rank
Rank at that position, that season, by total Opportunity volume. 1 = most opportunities.
Success Rate / Success Rank
Fantasy points earned per unit of Opportunity (efficiency, not volume). Success Rank only includes players with at least half the position's median Opportunity that season, so a couple of garbage-time touches don't vault someone to the top on a tiny sample.
+/- Lg Avg
Player's per-game average minus the position's league-wide weekly average, that season.
+/- Med
Player's per-game average minus the League Median row (median across the players currently shown in the table).
League Median row
Pinned row showing the median of each column among the currently filtered/displayed players.

Every numeric column is color-scaled green (best) to red (worst) within that column. Rank-style columns treat 1 (best rank) as green. Year checkboxes above the table toggle 2022-2026 in or out.

Draft ADP & Value table
ADP
Average draft position from your ADP/value sheet – lower number = drafted earlier.
Value
Auction-style $ value from the same sheet.
Stat Rank
The player's rank at that position, that season, in whichever stat is selected in the dropdown above (1 = best) – not the same ranking as Opp Rank/Success Rank, which are always Opportunity/efficiency-based.
$/Stat Pt
That year's Value ÷ the player's season TOTAL for the selected stat – literal cost per unit of production. Rounded to 2 decimals.

Click any column header to sort by it. The pinned row is the position's median draft $ per year. Cells are color-scaled (cheap $/pt and good ranks = green).

Volume vs. Consistency scatter

One dot per player, among players with a non-zero season total for the selected stat:

  • X (Volume Percentile): percentile rank of the player's season-total production.
  • Y (Consistency Percentile): a single "typical week" threshold is computed as the median of every weekly value posted by that qualifying group; each player's weeks-above-threshold count is then turned into a percentile the same way.
  • Dot color: draft $ from the ADP/value sheet – green = cheap, red = expensive.

With multiple years checked, X, Y, and dot color are each averaged across the seasons that player qualifies in. For 2026, X/Y use real weeks where available and projections for the rest; dot color uses that year's ADP sheet value.

Weekly Trend chart & table

Shows every week of every loaded season for whichever stat is selected in the dropdown next to the chart. For 2026, real weeks plot normally; projected weeks are drawn with an orange dashed line segment and an orange point, and the matching table cell is orange text.

Volume vs. Consistency by Season

Same X/Y/color definitions as the Position tab's version (see above), but plotted as one point per season for this single player, each computed against that season's full position field (not averaged together). A faint line connects the points in chronological order.

Compare Tabside-by-side player comparison

Pick a position and up to 3 players. For every stat with data for at least one of them:

  • Bar chart: vertical grouped bars, weeks 1-18 across 2024-2026 on the x-axis, one bar per player per week. This chart uses real weekly data only – it does not show 2026 projections.
  • Median table: each player's weekly median for that stat in 2024, 2025, and 2026 (2026 median is computed from whatever real weeks are loaded so far).

Player dropdowns are limited to players on a current (2026) roster.

General Glossary
PPR
Points Per Reception – a scoring format that awards fantasy points for each catch, in addition to yardage/touchdowns.
ADP
Average Draft Position – where a player is typically taken in a draft, averaged across drafts.
GP
Games Played.
CV / Volatility %
Coefficient of variation – standard deviation expressed as a percentage of the mean; a scale-independent measure of week-to-week consistency.
D/ST or DEF
Team defense/special teams, scored as a single fantasy "player" entry.
FA
Free agent – not currently on an NFL roster.
BYE
A week where a team doesn't play; shown as an opponent of "BYE" on the schedule.
